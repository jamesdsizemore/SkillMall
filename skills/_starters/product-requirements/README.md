# Product Requirements Document Starter

A PRD-writing skill grounded in the SVPG product vs. feature team model. Drives outcome ownership over output delivery, enforces problem-statement-first discipline, and produces both a full PRD and a lightweight one-pager.

## What's included

- SKILL.md with methodology drawn from [SVPG: Product vs. Feature Teams](https://www.svpg.com/product-vs-feature-teams/)
- `prd-template.md` — full PRD with eight structured sections
- `one-pager-template.md` — lightweight decision brief for early-stage alignment
- Fill-in markers for product name, success metrics, and launch date

## Fill-in markers

### [FILL-IN: github-username]
Your GitHub username for the `author` field in SKILL.md frontmatter.

### [FILL-IN: success-metrics]
The specific metrics you'll use to measure success. Example: "30-day retention rate ≥40%, NPS ≥50, activation rate ≥60%." Replace the placeholder in the Success Metrics section body.

### [FILL-IN: launch-date]
Target launch date and any hard constraints. Add this to the one-pager timeline field and the PRD launch criteria section.
