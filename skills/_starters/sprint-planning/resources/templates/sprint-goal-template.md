# Sprint Goal Template

A sprint goal is a single sentence that gives the sprint coherence and allows the team to make decisions during the sprint without re-planning.

---

## Format

> By end of [sprint dates], we will have [specific user-facing outcome], so that [business or user value].

---

## Checklist for a good sprint goal

- [ ] Single sentence (not a list of features)
- [ ] Describes an outcome, not a task list
- [ ] A user or customer would care about it
- [ ] Achievable within the sprint
- [ ] The team can make trade-offs to protect the goal if needed
- [ ] Agreed by the whole team, not just imposed by PO

---

## Examples

**Too task-oriented (bad):**
"Complete tickets #201, #202, and #205 in the backlog."

**Feature-oriented but no coherence (better):**
"Implement password reset and email verification."

**Outcome with business value (good):**
"A new user can sign up, verify their email, and deploy their first skill without contacting support."
