# Design Thinking Starter

A structured implementation of the Stanford d.school 5-stage design thinking process: Empathize, Define, Ideate, Prototype, Test. Includes empathy map and How Might We brainstorming templates.

## What's included

- SKILL.md with methodology drawn from the [Stanford d.school](https://dschool.stanford.edu/resources)
- `empathy-map.md` — four-quadrant empathy map (Says, Thinks, Does, Feels) plus Pains and Gains
- `how-might-we.md` — HMW question generation template with clustering worksheet
- Fill-in markers for problem domain and research participant criteria

## Fill-in markers

### [FILL-IN: github-username]
Your GitHub username for the `author` field in SKILL.md frontmatter.

### [FILL-IN: problem-domain]
The problem space you're applying design thinking to. Example: "reducing customer churn in the first 30 days" or "improving developer onboarding for a new SDK."

### [FILL-IN: research-participants]
Who to recruit for user research and how many. Example: "5-8 users who churned in the past 90 days and are willing to do a 45-minute interview" or "3-5 new hires from the last two onboarding cohorts."
