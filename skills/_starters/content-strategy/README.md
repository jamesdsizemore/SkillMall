# Content Strategy Starter

A content strategy planning skill that runs from audience definition through distribution and measurement. Built around the Content Marketing Institute's framework: audience and goal first, content types second, calendar third.

## What's included

- SKILL.md with methodology drawn from [Content Marketing Institute](https://contentmarketinginstitute.com/what-is-content-marketing/)
- `content-calendar.md` — monthly editorial calendar with status tracking
- `content-brief.md` — per-piece brief covering goal, audience, keyword, outline, and distribution
- Fill-in markers for target audience, content goal, and publishing cadence

## Fill-in markers

### [FILL-IN: github-username]
Your GitHub username for the `author` field in SKILL.md frontmatter.

### [FILL-IN: target-audience]
The specific audience persona this content strategy serves. Example: "Senior software engineers at companies with 50-500 employees who are evaluating observability tools and are skeptical of vendor lock-in."

### [FILL-IN: content-goal]
The primary business goal content must serve, stated as a measurable outcome. Example: "Generate 200 inbound demo requests per month from organic search" or "Establish thought leadership in the developer tooling space, measured by newsletter subscriber growth of 500/month."

### [FILL-IN: publishing-cadence]
Your actual publishing schedule. Example: "2 long-form posts per month, 3 short posts per month, 1 case study per quarter."
