## Days 1-30: Learn the System

**Philosophy:** Understand how work gets done here before proposing changes. Resist the urge to fix things until you understand why they are the way they are.

### Goals

1. Navigate the codebase and development workflow independently
2. Complete two assigned tasks with buddy support
3. Develop a working understanding of how the team prioritizes and ships work
4. Build relationships with your five most-important collaborators

### Deliverables

- [ ] Day one checklist completed
- [ ] First small task merged to production (by end of week 1)
- [ ] Second assigned task completed (by end of week 3)
- [ ] Attend two sprint ceremonies and be able to explain what happened in each
- [ ] Write a "what I've learned so far" document (private, for your own clarity)

### Key Relationships to Build

| Name | Role | Why they matter | Target: first 1:1 by |
|------|------|-----------------|----------------------|
| [Name] | Team lead | Direction and priorities | Week 1 |
| [Name] | Onboarding buddy | Daily support | Day 1 |
| [Name] | [Role] | [Why] | Week 2 |
| [Name] | [Role] | [Why] | Week 3 |

### Success Criteria

You're on track if by day 30 you can:
- Set up a development environment from scratch without help
- Describe the team's top three priorities and why they're prioritized that way
- Explain where your work fits in the product and the codebase

## Days 31-60: Contribute to the System

**Philosophy:** Operate independently on scoped work. Know when you're stuck and ask for help early — but identify the problem before asking.

### Goals

1. Own at least one task from kickoff through production, end-to-end
2. Participate actively in sprint planning with proposed estimates
3. Understand the team's on-call rotation and incident response process
4. Be the primary responder to one code review (not just a commenter)

### Deliverables

- [ ] One full task owned and shipped: [describe task here]
- [ ] PR reviewed for a teammate (substantive feedback, not just "LGTM")
- [ ] Shadow one incident or escalation (or read two postmortems)
- [ ] 30-day retrospective 1:1 with team lead completed

### Key Relationships to Build

- [Name] from [adjacent team] — understand their roadmap and how it intersects with yours
- [Name] from [role] — understand their perspective on what the team ships

### Success Criteria

You're on track if by day 60 you can:
- Take a task from ticket to production without step-by-step guidance
- Identify when a task is larger than estimated and raise it before it becomes a blocker
- Participate in sprint planning with informed estimates

---

## Days 61-90: Improve the System

**Philosophy:** Identify one thing the team does that could be done better and propose a change. This is how you demonstrate you've internalized the team's values, not just its processes.

### Goals

1. Identify one specific improvement to team process, tooling, or documentation
2. Propose it, get feedback, and implement it if approved
3. Be a reliable team member that others can depend on for scoped work
4. Develop a point of view on what the team should prioritize next quarter

### Deliverables

- [ ] Improvement proposal written and discussed with team lead: [describe it here when identified]
- [ ] Improvement implemented (or a decision made to defer it, with documented rationale)
- [ ] 60-day retrospective 1:1 with team lead completed
- [ ] 90-day retrospective 1:1 with team lead completed — discuss 6-month goals

### Success Criteria

You're on track if by day 90 you can:
- Be assigned a task and complete it with no more check-ins than any senior team member
- Articulate one concrete improvement and why it would help the team
- Name your top two priorities for the next quarter and why

---

## 90-Day Retrospective Questions

Answer these honestly before your 90-day 1:1:

1. What has been harder than expected? Why?
2. What has been easier than expected? Why?
3. What do you wish you had known on day one?
4. What would you change about this onboarding plan for the next person?
5. What do you want to focus on in the next 90 days?
